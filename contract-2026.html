    How does the Railway Labor Act process work?
    Airline contracts never expire. They become “amendable.” Negotiations continue until an agreement is reached or the National Mediation Board releases the parties. [Expand with MEC-approved copy.]

    Will every pilot get to vote on a TA?
    Yes. Any tentative agreement the MEC approves goes to the full pilot group for a ratification vote, after road shows and full TA materials.
